Manage Your Booking

How to Book

1. Go to the Flights page and choose your flight.

2. Click Book Now and fill in passenger details.

3. Pay online via UPI, Card, or Net Banking.

4. You will receive a confirmation on your email.

Cancellation Policy

Cancel 48 hours before departure: Full refund.

Cancel 24 hours before departure: 50% refund.

Cancel less than 24 hours: No refund.

Track Your Booking

Use your booking ID received on email to track status.

Contact support at support@airlines.com for help.
